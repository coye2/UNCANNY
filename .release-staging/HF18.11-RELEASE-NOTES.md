# UNCANNY v0.20.0-alpha.1 — ELYSIUM Engine 4.5 — HF18.11

HF18.11 fixes a direct-D3D11 neural promotion bug uncovered while reviewing the latest Fallout/Stray stability reports and runtime code.

## What was actually wrong

HF18.9 correctly made the direct-D3D11 current-frame path universal to stop the hard-lock class, but the compatibility floor was also acting as a permanent neural kill switch: direct D3D11 always entered the safe floor, forced DLSS5 off for every frame, and prevented the maintenance thread from ever warming D3D11 neural resources.

That explains the tester-visible state where ordinary UNCANNY/ELYSIUM could work in a D3D11 title while DLSS5 never became live.

## HF18.11 staged D3D11 neural promotion

- Keeps the universal current-frame D3D11 safety floor instead of reverting the hard-lock repair.
- Requires **240 successful ELYSIUM frames** plus an advancing/recent native Present stream and at least **9 seconds** since the active DXGI transition before direct-D3D11 neural promotion is eligible.
- D3D11 neural/shared-resource initialization remains on the maintenance thread; Present never waits behind provider/resource setup.
- Native depth, optical-flow temporal history, and the risky advanced temporal route remain deferred on the direct-D3D11 safe path even after neural becomes eligible.
- If the neural provider is unavailable, busy, broken, or still warming, the live source frame is retained.
- Resize/fullscreen/color-space transitions reset D3D11 post-processing progress and revoke neural promotion until the route stabilizes again.
- The policy is universal and contains no Fallout 4, Stray, or other executable-name exception.

## Preserved HF18.10 / PCSX2 behavior

- HF18.10's route-wide direct-D3D12 startup and ResizeBuffers/ResizeBuffers1 protection is unchanged.
- PCSX2 remains explicitly on Direct3D 12 with `Renderer=15`.
- Verified sidecar launch, Cloud Files-aware PCSX2 handling, legacy-wrapper migration, installer fast paths, rollback, scanner/library fixes, REVENANT, Motion Guard, and Ghosting Guard remain preserved.
