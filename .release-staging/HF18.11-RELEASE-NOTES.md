# UNCANNY v0.20.0-alpha.1 — ELYSIUM Engine 4.5 — HF18.11

HF18.11 fixes the D3D11 neural path without undoing the stability work from the last few builds.

The problem was pretty simple: the D3D11 safe floor kept games stable, but it also kept neural processing disabled forever. ELYSIUM could be running normally while the neural route never got a chance to come online.

## What changed

Direct D3D11 now starts on the conservative current-frame path. After the route has stayed healthy for 240 processed frames and at least 9 seconds since the last relevant DXGI transition, neural resources are allowed to warm up and the neural path can become eligible.

The riskier direct-depth and temporal route is still held back on this safe path. Resize, fullscreen and similar transitions reset the promotion window.

HF18.10's D3D12 startup/resize fix is unchanged, and PCSX2 stays on Direct3D 12 with `Renderer=15`.

The current sidecar launcher, old PCSX2 migration, scanner/manual-add fixes, rollback, REVENANT, Motion Guard and Ghosting Guard are all still there.
