UNCANNY

Universal DLSS 5 Neural Remaster Engine for PC Games and Emulators.

UNCANNY is an independent Windows real-time remaster middleware project combining DLSS 5 / Feature-18 neural rendering, ELYSIUM multi-pass reconstruction, Motion Guard, Ghosting Guard, REVENANT persistent asset reconstruction, legacy-game compatibility, diagnostics and rollback.

Hotfix 10

The HOME-page UNCANNY PASSES selector now drives the native ELYSIUM reconstruction stack at 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2.5 / 3, independently from the separate DLSS 5 pass control. ELYSIUM advanced reconstruction sliders are wired through the live renderer path.

Core systems

  • ELYSIUM reconstruction with live multi-pass depth and advanced structural/material controls.
  • DLSS 5 neural rendering with create/evaluate/return/present evidence.
  • Motion Guard + Ghosting Guard for temporal stability.
  • REVENANT persistent asset reconstruction.
  • Legacy compatibility including 32-bit/x86 neural-helper work.
  • Control Deck, diagnostics, backups and rollback.

Compatibility

Direct3D 12 and D3D11 are primary PC paths. PCSX2 is the primary emulator target. D3D9/D3D10 neural behavior remains experimental, and Vulkan/OpenGL are not yet at DirectX parity.
